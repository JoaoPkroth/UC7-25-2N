1) Escreva 5 frases utilizando a preposição IN. Use as três aplicações do 'IN' dentro destas frases.

I live in New York City.
She was born in 1995.
The documents are in the folder on my desk.
He enjoys swimming in the ocean.
The cat is sleeping in the sun.


2) Escreva 5 frases utilizando a preposição ON. Use as tres aplicações do ON dentro destas frases.
 
The book is on the table.
They will meet on Friday for lunch.
She relies on her friends for support.
The painting hangs on the wall.
He was on the bus when it started to rain.

3) Escreva 5 frases utilizando a preposição 'AT'. Use as quatro aplicações do 'AT' dentro destas frases. 

The class starts **at 8 a.m.**
The error is **at line 25**.
You can find the answer **at page 42**.
We met **at the school festival**.
I usually wake up **at 7 a.m.**

4) Escreva 5 frases utilizando a preposição 'OF'. Escreva casos com of sozinho e tambem acompanhado de 'this' ou 'the'.

This book is one of the most interesting novels I've ever read.
She is a member of the committee that organized the event.
The color of this wall doesn't match the furniture.
He drank a cup of coffee before starting work.
The end of the movie surprised everyone in the theater.
